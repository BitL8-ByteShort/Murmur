# Contribution licensing

A separate contributor license agreement is no longer required for new
contributions to Murmur. Submit new contributions under the project's
GPL-3.0-only license, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
Contributors retain ownership of their work.

The previous proprietary-release CLA is retained in Git history for historical
records. This change does not rewrite agreements previously accepted or change
the license of separately identified third-party material.
